# Data review list

The dictionary in `src/data/dictionary.json` was merged from two sources (the original `kamus-ogan.json` and the curated `kamus-bahase-ogan.html`).
For the words below the two sources give **different meanings**. The meaning kept in the app is the one from the curated HTML version; the other is listed here so you can decide.

Edit the entry in `src/data/dictionary.json` (the `indo` list) after checking the printed dictionary by Joni Faisal.
